FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you start a feasibility study?+
We can typically mobilize within 2–4 weeks of contract signing. For scoping studies, we can often start within 1 week. We will provide a detailed project schedule and resource plan as part of our proposal.
Do you work on projects outside Iran?+
Yes, we have delivered projects in 30+ countries including Kazakhstan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Australia, Canada, South Africa, and across Latin America. Our Dubai, Perth, and Toronto offices support international projects.
What is the typical cost of a BFS?+
BFS costs vary significantly based on project size, complexity, and location. Typically, a BFS costs 0.5–1.5% of the estimated project CAPEX. For a $500M project, this would be $2.5M–$7.5M. We provide detailed proposals with fixed-fee or time-and-materials options.
Can you provide a Qualified Person (QP) for NI 43-101 reports?+
Yes, we have NI 43-101 Qualified Persons (QPs) on staff for geology, mining, metallurgy, and geotechnical disciplines. We can also sign off on JORC 2012 Competent Person reports for ASX-listed companies.
Do you offer free initial consultations?+
Yes, we offer a free 60-minute initial consultation to understand your project requirements and discuss how we can help. Use the contact form above or email us at info@kdmine.com to schedule a call.
