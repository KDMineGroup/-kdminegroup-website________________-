Global Mining Equipment Market Outlook Q2 2026
Published: June 10, 2026 • KDMine Research
The global mining equipment market continues to face extended lead times in Q2 2026, with major OEMs reporting 18-24 month delivery schedules for large haul trucks and excavators. Supply chain constraints, particularly for electronic components and specialized steel castings, remain the primary bottleneck. Chinese manufacturers have increased market share in the 100-200 ton haul truck segment, offering 30-40% cost savings with 8-12 month delivery times. However, concerns about parts availability and service networks persist in remote mining regions. Copper and gold price strength is driving increased CAPEX commitments, particularly in South America and West Africa, putting additional pressure on equipment availability. We recommend early equipment procurement (24-30 months before required on-site date) and consideration of alternative suppliers for non-critical fleet components.
