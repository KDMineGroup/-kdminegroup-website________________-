FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between PFS and BFS?+
A PFS provides ±30% accuracy for investment decisions. A BFS provides ±20% accuracy, is NI 43-101/JORC compliant, and is required for project financing and board approval.
How long does a BFS take?+
A typical BFS takes 12–18 months. Scoping studies can be completed in 4–8 weeks, while PFS studies typically take 6–12 months.
What data do you need to start?+
We need geological drill hole data, assay results, topographic survey, metallurgical testwork, and existing reports. For BFS, geotechnical data and environmental baseline studies are also required.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between PFS and BFS?+
A PFS provides ±30% accuracy for investment decisions. A BFS provides ±20% accuracy, is NI 43-101/JORC compliant, and is required for project financing and board approval.
How long does a BFS take?+
A typical BFS takes 12–18 months. Scoping studies can be completed in 4–8 weeks, while PFS studies typically take 6–12 months.
What data do you need to start?+
We need geological drill hole data, assay results, topographic survey, metallurgical testwork, and existing reports. For BFS, geotechnical data and environmental baseline studies are also required.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between PFS and BFS?+
A PFS provides ±30% accuracy for investment decisions. A BFS provides ±20% accuracy, is NI 43-101/JORC compliant, and is required for project financing and board approval.
How long does a BFS take?+
A typical BFS takes 12–18 months. Scoping studies can be completed in 4–8 weeks, while PFS studies typically take 6–12 months.
What data do you need to start?+
We need geological drill hole data, assay results, topographic survey, metallurgical testwork, and existing reports. For BFS, geotechnical data and environmental baseline studies are also required.
