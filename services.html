Our Mining Engineering Services

Comprehensive solutions from equipment optimization to production excellence, backed by 50+ years of combined international expertise and AACE standards compliance

Complete Mining Solutions

At Kavian Development Mine, we offer end-to-end mining engineering services that cover every aspect of your project lifecycle. From initial equipment selection and international procurement to comprehensive plant engineering and ongoing production optimization, our veteran team delivers world-class solutions tailored to your specific needs.

01

Equipment Optimization & Plant Design

Maximize efficiency and minimize costs with our advanced equipment selection and plant layout optimization services. Our team analyzes your specific operational requirements, ore characteristics, and production targets to recommend the most suitable equipment and optimal plant configuration.

  • Comprehensive equipment selection based on ore characteristics and production capacity
  • 3D plant layout design and workflow optimization
  • Energy efficiency analysis and sustainability recommendations
  • Equipment sizing and capacity matching for optimal throughput
  • Integration of automation and control systems
Expert Design
02

International Equipment Procurement

Leverage our extensive global network to source the best mining equipment at competitive prices. We connect you with verified manufacturers and suppliers across five continents, handling all aspects of procurement from initial negotiations to final delivery and commissioning.

  • Direct access to verified manufacturers in Europe, Americas, Asia, and Australia
  • Competitive bidding and price negotiation on your behalf
  • Quality assurance and factory inspection services
  • Complete logistics management and customs clearance support
  • After-sales support and spare parts procurement
Global Network
03

Cost Analysis & Real-Time Equipment Pricing

Make informed investment decisions with our comprehensive CAPEX and OPEX analysis services. We provide access to our proprietary real-time equipment pricing database, updated daily from global markets, ensuring you have the most accurate cost information for your project planning and budgeting.

  • AACE-compliant cost estimation for all project phases (Class 5 to Class 1)
  • Real-time equipment pricing from global suppliers and manufacturers
  • Detailed CAPEX breakdown including equipment, installation, and infrastructure
  • OPEX modeling with labor, energy, consumables, and maintenance costs
  • Cost optimization recommendations and value engineering
Real-Time Data
04

Feasibility Studies & Financial Consulting

Secure project funding and ensure viability with our comprehensive feasibility studies that meet international banking and investment standards. Our studies follow AACE guidelines and include detailed technical, economic, and financial analysis to support your investment decisions and financing applications.

  • Preliminary Economic Assessment (PEA) and Scoping Studies
  • Pre-Feasibility Studies (PFS) and Bankable Feasibility Studies (BFS)
  • Economic modeling including NPV, IRR, and payback period analysis
  • Risk assessment and sensitivity analysis
  • Financial structuring and funding strategy development
AACE Compliant
05

Plant Engineering & Production Optimization

From initial concept to operational excellence, we provide complete plant engineering services that maximize your production output while minimizing operational costs. Our team handles every phase including detailed engineering, construction supervision, commissioning, and ongoing optimization to ensure your plant operates at peak efficiency.

  • Conceptual and detailed engineering design (P&IDs, equipment specifications)
  • Construction management and quality control oversight
  • Commissioning, start-up assistance, and performance testing
  • Process optimization and debottlenecking studies
  • Ongoing technical support and continuous improvement programs
Full Lifecycle
