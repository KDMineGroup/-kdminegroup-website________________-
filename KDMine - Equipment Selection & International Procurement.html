Equipment Selection
Sizing & Selection Methodology

Equipment selection in open-pit mining begins with the mine plan. Truck and shovel fleet size is determined by the annual material movement (ore + waste), haul distances, cycle times, and availability factors. Process plant equipment is sized from the process flowsheet — throughput, grind size, recovery targets, and circuit configuration.

KDMine uses a structured selection matrix that evaluates OEM options on capital cost, operating cost, availability, parts support, and local serviceability. For Iranian and CIS projects, we additionally assess import feasibility, sanctions compliance, and local substitute availability.

1
Mine Plan Input
Annual material movement, haul profile, bench height, and pit geometry define fleet requirements.
2
Fleet Sizing Model
Truck-shovel matching model calculates required fleet size with productivity and availability factors.
3
OEM Shortlist
2–4 OEM options shortlisted per equipment type based on size class, regional support, and budget.
4
Selection Matrix
Weighted scoring across CAPEX, OPEX, availability, parts, lead time, and local support.
5
Budget Quotation
Budget quotations obtained from preferred OEMs and integrated into the CAPEX estimate.

Global Sourcing Strategy

International procurement for mining projects involves coordinating equipment purchases across multiple OEM countries, managing long lead times, and navigating import regulations. KDMine's procurement advisory service provides cost modelling for the full landed cost — ex-works price, freight, insurance, import duties, port handling, and inland transport.

For Iranian and CIS projects, our Tehran-based team provides specialist knowledge of local import channels, approved vendor lists, and alternative equipment sources from China, Russia, and Eastern Europe that comply with project requirements.

International Freight & Inland Transport
🚢
Ocean Freight
Break-bulk or RoRo for large equipment. Container for smaller items. Port of loading to port of discharge.
2–6% of Ex-Works
✈️
Air Freight
Critical spare parts and instruments only. High cost but essential for minimising downtime on operating mines.
8–15× Ocean Rate
🚛
Inland Transport
Heavy-haul trucking for oversized loads. Route surveys, permits, and escorts required for large equipment.
1–8% of Ex-Works
🏗️
Port Handling
Stevedoring, storage, and port authority fees. Highly variable by port. Congestion surcharges in peak periods.
0.5–2% of Ex-Works
