What Is CAPEX in Mining?

Capital expenditure (CAPEX) in open-pit mining covers all one-time costs required to bring a mine from greenfield to first production. This includes direct costs (equipment, civil, structural, mechanical, electrical, piping) and indirect costs (engineering, procurement, construction management, owner's costs, and contingency).

KDMine prepares CAPEX estimates across all AACE classes — from Class 5 parametric models (±50%) to Class 2 definitive estimates with firm vendor quotes (±10%). Each estimate is built on a structured WBS aligned to industry standard account codes.
