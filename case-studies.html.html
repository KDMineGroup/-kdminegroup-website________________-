Rio Mining Saves $2.4M Through Fleet Optimization

Rio Mining Corporation • Chile

Comprehensive haul truck fleet analysis and replacement strategy reduced operating costs by 18% while improving productivity by 22% at a major copper operation.

Challenge

Rio Mining operated a mixed fleet of 45 haul trucks (Caterpillar 793F and Komatsu 930E) with escalating maintenance costs and declining availability. The mine faced:

  • Average truck age of 12 years with 35,000+ operating hours
  • Mechanical availability dropped from 87% to 71% over 3 years
  • Maintenance costs increased 45% year-over-year
  • Production targets missed by 12% due to equipment downtime
  • Fuel consumption 15% above industry benchmarks

Our Approach

Kavian deployed a 4-person team for a comprehensive 8-week analysis:

Data Collection

3 years historical data

Fleet Analysis

Lifecycle cost modeling

Optimization

Replacement strategy

Procurement

Vendor negotiations

We analyzed maintenance records, fuel consumption, production data, and conducted time studies. Our proprietary AI model predicted optimal replacement timing for each unit.

Solution Implemented

  • Phase 1 (Year 1): Replace 12 oldest trucks with new Caterpillar 794 AC units
  • Phase 2 (Year 2): Major component rebuilds on 18 mid-life trucks
  • Phase 3 (Year 3): Retire remaining 15 oldest units, standardize on Cat 794 platform
  • Implemented predictive maintenance program using telematics data
  • Negotiated fleet purchase agreement saving $3.2M vs. list pricing
  • Established parts inventory optimization reducing carrying costs by $450K

Key Innovation: Our AI-powered lifecycle cost model analyzed 127 variables to determine optimal replacement timing, achieving 94% prediction accuracy compared to actual outcomes.

Results & Impact

After 18 months of implementation:

  • $2.4M annual cost savings (18% reduction in total fleet operating costs)
  • Fleet mechanical availability increased from 71% to 89%
  • Maintenance costs per operating hour reduced by 32%
  • Fuel consumption improved 12% through newer, more efficient engines
  • Production exceeded targets by 8% due to improved availability
  • Safety incidents reduced 35% with newer equipment and better training

Challenge

Andean Copper needed to replace aging crushing equipment but received a single quote of $35.5M from their incumbent supplier. They sought competitive alternatives without compromising quality or delivery.

Our Approach

  • Developed detailed technical specifications and performance requirements
  • Invited bids from 5 qualified manufacturers (Metso, FLSmidth, Sandvik, ThyssenKrupp, CITIC)
  • Conducted technical evaluation scoring system (40% technical, 30% commercial, 30% delivery)
  • Negotiated terms including performance guarantees and payment schedules
  • Managed logistics, customs clearance, and installation supervision

Results

  • Final contract: $27.0M with FLSmidth (24% savings vs. initial quote)
  • Equipment delivered on schedule with performance guarantees met
  • Crusher throughput 8% above nameplate capacity in first year
  • Established framework for future competitive procurement
