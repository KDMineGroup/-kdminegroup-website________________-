Our Approach
Procurement Process
A structured, proven methodology that ensures optimal procurement outcomes for your mining project.
1
📋
Needs Assessment
Define procurement requirements, specifications, quantities, delivery schedules, and budget constraints. Develop procurement strategy aligned with project timeline and cash flow.
2
🔍
Market Research
Identify potential suppliers, analyze market conditions, benchmark pricing, assess lead times, and evaluate geopolitical risks. Build comprehensive supplier shortlist.
3
📨
Tendering & Evaluation
Prepare RFQ/RFP packages, conduct bidder conferences, receive and evaluate proposals on technical, commercial, and delivery criteria. Conduct reference checks and site visits.
4
✍️
Negotiation & Award
Negotiate final terms, pricing, payment conditions, warranties, and delivery schedules. Finalize contracts, establish performance bonds, and coordinate mobilization activities.
