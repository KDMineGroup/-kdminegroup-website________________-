Process Plant Design
From Flowsheet to Plant Layout

KDMine's plant design service begins with the process flowsheet — the engineering document that defines how ore is transformed into a saleable product. The flowsheet drives equipment selection, sizing, and ultimately the process plant CAPEX and OPEX. Our team works from metallurgical test work results to develop a practical, cost-optimised circuit design.

For each circuit, we evaluate multiple design options — throughput capacity, energy efficiency, water consumption, and reagent usage — and present a comparative cost analysis to support the optimal design selection.

Financial Modelling
DCF Model & Project Economics

KDMine builds fully integrated discounted cash flow (DCF) financial models for mining projects. The model integrates the mine plan, process plant throughput, CAPEX schedule, OPEX profile, commodity price assumptions, royalties, taxes, and working capital to produce project-level NPV, IRR, and payback metrics.

All models are built in Excel with full audit trail, scenario manager (base/upside/downside), and Monte Carlo simulation capability. Bilingual EN/FA versions available for Iranian project submissions.
