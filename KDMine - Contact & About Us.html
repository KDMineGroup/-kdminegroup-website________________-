Mining Cost Expertise Built Over Two Decades

KDMine was founded by a team of AACE-certified cost engineers and mining professionals with a shared mission: to bring institutional-quality cost estimation to mining projects of all sizes, in any geography.

From a single-geologist startup in 2006 to a 25-person global consultancy in 2026, KDMine has delivered over 40 cost estimates across 6 continents, covering $12B+ in total CAPEX.

Our Story
KDMine Timeline
2006
Founded in Vancouver
KDMine established as a sole-practitioner cost consultancy focused on Canadian open-pit gold projects.
2011
First International Project
Delivered first Class 3 estimate for a copper project in Kazakhstan. CIS regional expertise established.
2015
Perth Office Opens
Australian operations launched with Sarah Reynolds joining as Senior Process Plant Estimator. First West Africa project delivered.
2018
Tehran Office & Iran Expansion
Mohammad Hosseini joins as Iran & CIS Regional Specialist. Bilingual EN/FA reporting capability launched. First NI 43-101 compliant report for Iranian client.
2021
$10B CAPEX Milestone
Cumulative CAPEX estimated by KDMine surpasses $10 billion across 35 projects. Monte Carlo simulation capability added.
2024
KDMine Platform Launch
Launch of the KDMine digital platform — interactive cost tools, live index data, and the industry's first bilingual EN/FA mining cost estimation portal.
2026
25 Team Members · 40+ Projects · 6 Continents
KDMine today — a globally recognised mining cost consultancy with offices in Vancouver, Tehran, and Perth, serving clients across 6 continents.
