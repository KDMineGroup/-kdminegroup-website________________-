About Kavian Development Mine

Our Story

Kavian Development Mine was founded in 2024 by a team of seasoned mining engineers who recognized a gap in the industry - the need for a consultancy that combines decades of proven expertise with modern, innovative approaches.

Our founding team brings over 50 years of combined international experience in mining equipment optimization, plant engineering, and project management. We've worked on projects across five continents, from small-scale operations to multi-billion dollar mining complexes.

What sets us apart is our fresh perspective backed by deep industry knowledge. We leverage cutting-edge technology, real-time global market intelligence, and an extensive international network to deliver cost-effective, efficient solutions for our clients.
