Estimation Techniques
Methodology by Estimate Type

📐 Factored / Parametric Methods

⚡
Lang Factor MethodApplied to Class 4–5 estimates. Total CAPEX = Delivered Equipment Cost × Lang Factor (3.1–4.7 for mining).
📊
Hand Factor MethodBreaks Lang factor into 9 components. More accurate for Class 3–4. Accounts for installation, piping, instrumentation.
📈
Capacity Factoring (6/10 Rule)Scale cost from known project: C₂ = C₁ × (Q₂/Q₁)^0.6. Used for throughput scaling in Class 4–5.

🔢 Detailed / Bottom-Up Methods

📋
Equipment-Based EstimateClass 2–3 method. Each major equipment item priced from vendor quotes or CostMine database. Installation factors applied per discipline.
🏗️
Unit Rate MethodCivil/structural quantities × unit rates ($/m³, $/t steel). Rates sourced from regional contractor databases and RSMeans.
📦
Material Take-Off (MTO)Class 1–2 method. Full BOQ from engineering drawings. Firm contractor quotes applied to each line item.

⛏️ Mining OPEX Components

🚛
Equipment Operating CostFuel, tires, maintenance, operator wages. Sourced from CostMine Equipment Cost database. Applied per equipment hour × utilization.
💥
Drill & Blast CostDrilling ($/m), explosives (ANFO/emulsion $/t rock), initiation systems. Benchmarked against comparable operations.
👷
Labour CostCrew size × shift schedule × wage rate (including on-costs). Regional labour rates from CostMine and local surveys.

🏭 Processing OPEX Components

⚡
Power CostSpecific power consumption (kWh/t) × throughput × power tariff ($/kWh). Benchmarked against similar processing routes.
🧪
Reagent & ConsumablesReagent consumption rates (g/t or kg/t) × unit cost. Grinding media, liners, flotation reagents, cyanide, acid.
🔧
Maintenance & G&AMaintenance = % of CAPEX (1.5–4%). G&A includes site management, camp, environmental monitoring, security.

⚠️ Risk Identification

📋
Risk RegisterStructured identification of cost risks using AACE RP 40R-08. Each risk assigned probability, impact, and mitigation strategy.
🎲
Monte Carlo SimulationProbabilistic cost model using @Risk or Crystal Ball. P10/P50/P90 cost outcomes generated for each estimate class.
🔍
Sensitivity AnalysisTornado chart identifies top 5 cost drivers. Typically: equipment cost, labour productivity, strip ratio, power cost, exchange rate.

🛡️ Contingency Determination

📊
AACE RP 44R-08 MethodContingency determined from estimate quality score (EQS). Higher EQS = lower contingency requirement.
📐
Expected Value MethodContingency = Σ(Risk Probability × Risk Impact). Applied to each identified risk in the risk register.
🏆
Benchmarking MethodContingency compared against historical outturn data from 40+ KDMine projects and published industry databases.

📈 Cost Escalation Methods

📊
Index-Based EscalationCost₂ = Cost₁ × (Index₂ / Index₁). Uses MCCI, CEPCI, or US PPI depending on cost component type.
🌍
Location Factor MethodRegional cost adjustment: Cost_Region = Cost_Base × Location Factor. Factors sourced from CostMine, RSMeans, and KDMine proprietary database.
💱
Currency AdjustmentMulti-currency estimates use forward exchange rate curves. Local content split (USD vs local currency) explicitly modelled.

🗓️ Escalation During Construction

📅
S-Curve Cash FlowCAPEX spend profile modelled as S-curve over construction period. Escalation applied to each year's spend using forecast indices.
🔢
Weighted EscalationDifferent escalation rates for labour (CPI), equipment (PPI), and materials (steel/copper indices). Weighted by cost component split.
⚡
Owner's Cost EscalationEPCM fees, owner's team, permits, and studies escalated separately using professional services CPI indices.
