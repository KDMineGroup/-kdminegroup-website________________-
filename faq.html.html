Cost Estimation & Pricing

How accurate are your cost estimations?

Our cost estimations follow AACE International standards with accuracy ranging from ±50% (Class 5) for early studies to ±10% (Class 1) for detailed engineering. Our AI-powered models achieve 94% accuracy in predictions, significantly above industry averages.

What is included in CAPEX and OPEX analysis?

CAPEX includes equipment costs, installation, infrastructure, engineering, and contingencies. OPEX covers labor, energy, consumables, maintenance, and overhead. We provide detailed breakdowns with sensitivity analysis and optimization recommendations for both categories.

How long does a cost estimation study take?

Timeline varies by complexity: Class 5 (Order of Magnitude) takes 1-2 weeks, Class 3 (Budget) takes 4-6 weeks, and Class 1 (Definitive) takes 8-12 weeks. Rush services are available for urgent projects.
